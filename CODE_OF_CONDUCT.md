# Code of Conduct

hyperplot is a small project, and we'd like it to stay a pleasant place to
work. So:

- **Be kind.** Disagree with ideas, not people. No harassment, insults or
  personal attacks, in any space the project uses.
- **Assume good faith.** Most people asking a question have already tried
  the obvious thing. Most people closing your PR have a reason; ask what it
  is.
- **Keep it on topic.** Issues and pull requests are for hyperplot. Take
  flame wars about frameworks elsewhere.
- **Respect privacy.** Don't publish other people's personal information.

The maintainer may edit, hide or delete comments, close issues or PRs, and
block people who don't follow this. They'll usually say why.

## Reporting

If someone is making the project unpleasant, tell the maintainer,
[@ironystock](https://github.com/ironystock). If the matter isn't sensitive,
mention them in an issue. For anything private, use the contact links on
their GitHub profile; GitHub itself has no direct messages. Reports are kept
confidential.

Security problems are a different channel: see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
